Key findings

  • Retention is the biggest lever, not acquisition. 97% of customers place exactly one order, and month-1 repeat rate is 0.48% — the business runs almost entirely on new-customer acquisition.
  • Revenue concentration is real. High-value segments (50.0% of customers) generate 83.3% of revenue, but Low-Value One-Time customers still contribute 16.5% on volume alone.
  • A small set of sellers carries outsized delivery risk. The top-ranked seller has a 38.5% cancellation rate on just 13 orders — cancellations are weighted 2x in the risk score as a stronger reliability signal than late delivery.
  • Recommendation: prioritize a win-back campaign for the 23,078 "Lapsed High-Value" customers (R$5.46M in historical revenue) before further acquisition spend, and flag sellers with risk score >70 for manual review.
